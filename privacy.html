Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Interface AI ("we", "us") collects through this website and how we use it. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you write in your message, such as details about your organisation or migration project.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to operate and secure the website.

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and follow up on conversations you start with us.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Please don't send sensitive data

When describing a migration challenge, please do not email us real customer records, policy data, health or financial information, or other confidential data. A description of the systems and formats involved is all we need to start a conversation.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run this website and our email (for example our web hosting and email providers), and only as needed for those services, or where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting business relationship, and then delete them. Server logs are retained according to our hosting provider's standard retention period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email contact@interfaceai.io.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products develop. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@interfaceai.io.
