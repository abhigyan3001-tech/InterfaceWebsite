How it works

From source to destination in four steps.

01 / INGEST

Connect or upload

Receive source datasets from files, exports or supported system integrations.

02 / MAP

Understand the data

Identify source fields and align them with destination requirements.

03 / TRANSFORM

Convert & validate

Apply mapping rules, normalize formats and flag inconsistencies.

04 / DELIVER

Export or integrate

Produce the structure required by the destination platform.

About us

Building bridges between systems.

Interface AI is an early-stage technology initiative focused on making data portable, understandable and usable across platforms. We believe migration should be guided by clear rules, transparent validation and human oversight—not tied to one legacy system or industry.

Our direction is to combine automation with intelligent data interpretation so teams can move information with greater confidence and less manual rework.
